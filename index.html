What is RinSaathi?

RinSaathi Advisory Services is an independent loan, debt and credit advisory service that provides guidance and education to help customers understand their options.

Does RinSaathi provide new loans?

No. RinSaathi is not a loan provider and does not provide new loans.

Does RinSaathi guarantee loan settlement?

No. We do not guarantee settlement, waiver, approval or any particular outcome. Guidance depends on the individual situation and the relevant lender's decisions.

Can RinSaathi help with CIBIL-related concerns?

Yes. We can provide guidance on understanding credit-report information, account status and possible next steps.

Can I get guidance about recovery calls?

Yes. We can guide you on documenting communications and responding in a calm, organised and informed manner.

Is my information confidential?
